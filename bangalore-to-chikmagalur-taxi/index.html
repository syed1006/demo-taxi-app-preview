The Bangalore–Chikmagalur Drive

Karnataka's coffee heartland is a 245 km run on NH-75 — out through Nelamangala, Kunigal and Channarayapatna to Hassan, then the Belur road into the hills. Leave by 6 am and you clear the Nelamangala stretch before truck traffic builds, reaching Chikmagalur town for a late lunch even with a breakfast stop.

The Hoysala temples at Belur and Halebidu sit right on the corridor, and their carved friezes deserve two or three hours en route. Above the town, the road climbs toward Mullayanagiri — at 1,930 metres the highest peak in Karnataka — and on steep ghat pulls like this the AC stays off, as it does on all our hill routes.

Two days is the sensible minimum: day one for the drive and the Belur–Halebidu stop, day two for Mullayanagiri, Baba Budangiri and a coffee-estate walk before the return. Hebbe Falls near Kemmangundi needs a local 4x4 for the final stretch; your cab drops you at the jeep point.

Top Places to Visit in Chikmagalur

Mullayanagiri Peak

Karnataka's highest point at 1,930 m, with a small hilltop temple and huge Western Ghats views.

Baba Budangiri

Sacred peak and shrine where coffee first arrived in India, ringed by grassy ridgelines.

Coffee Museum

Quick primer on how Chikmagalur's estates grow, pulp and roast their beans.

Hirekolale Lake

Calm reservoir just outside town with mirror reflections of the Mullayanagiri range at sunset.

Kemmangundi & Hebbe Falls

Hill station gardens and a two-stage waterfall reached by shared 4x4 from the jeep point.

Belur Chennakeshava Temple

Star-shaped Hoysala masterpiece en route, its bracket figures among India's finest carving.

Halebidu Hoysaleswara Temple

The old Hoysala capital's twin-shrined temple, wrapped in bands of sculpted friezes.

Travel Tips

  • Leave Bangalore by 6 am to clear Nelamangala before truck traffic builds on NH-75.
  • Stop at Belur and Halebidu on the way in — the Hoysala carvings deserve at least two hours.
  • AC stays off on the Mullayanagiri and Baba Budangiri climbs, as on all steep ghat sections.
  • Hebbe Falls needs a shared local 4x4 from the Kemmangundi jeep point; budget ₹400–500 per head.
  • Carry leech protection if you plan monsoon treks — the trails are famously leechy from June to September.

Bangalore to Chikmagalur — FAQs

How long does Bangalore to Chikmagalur take by taxi?

Around five and a half hours for the 245 km via NH-75 and Hassan, including a breakfast halt. A 6 am start gets you into town for a late lunch even with a Belur stop.

Can we visit Belur and Halebidu on the way?

Yes — both sit just off the route between Hassan and Chikmagalur and add two to three hours to day one. It is the natural way to see them without a separate trip.

Does the cab stay with us for local sightseeing?

Yes, on the two-day round trip the driver stays overnight and covers your local circuit — fuel and driver bata are included, with a 300 km/day cap and extra kilometres billed per the rate card. Tolls, parking and entry fees are extra.

Can the cab go up to Mullayanagiri?

The tarred road runs to a car park near the summit, from where a short flight of steps leads to the peak temple. The climb is steep and narrow, and the AC stays off on this stretch per hill policy.

How do we reach Hebbe Falls?

Regular cabs cannot do the last stretch — it is a rough track from Kemmangundi covered by shared local 4x4 jeeps, roughly ₹400–500 per head. Your cab drops and picks you up at the jeep point.
