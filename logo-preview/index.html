Logo Concepts

Four directions in the heritage identity. Each shows the header lockup on light and dark, and the square mark at app-icon, favicon and browser-tab sizes. Toggle the site's dark mode (header button) to see how each behaves in context.

Gopura Badge

Gold stepped temple-tower on a kempu→maroon field, cab beneath — the most heritage-forward mark; reads beautifully as an app icon.

Zari Seal

Maroon roundel with double gold zari rings and a BUC serif monogram — the most premium/formal; strong on invoices and cards.

Kempu-Haladi Road

The Kannada flag's gold-over-red field with a highway winding through — the boldest, most literal Karnataka statement.

Velocity Chevrons
