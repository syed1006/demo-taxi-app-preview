Hire-a-Driver FAQs

Are your drivers experienced with automatic cars?

Yes — tell us your car model when booking and we assign a driver experienced with automatics, manuals, EVs or premium cars accordingly.

How are the drivers verified?

Every acting driver carries a valid commercial licence, has police verification on file, and is personally vetted with a driving assessment before joining our roster.

Who pays for fuel and tolls when I hire just a driver?

You do — it's your car. You pay only the fixed driver charge, plus his return travel for late-night or outstation duties (confirmed upfront).

Can I hire a driver for an outstation trip in my car?

Yes. Outstation duties are ₹1,199 per day plus the driver's food and stay (or a fixed bata if you prefer). Multi-day trips and one-way relocations are both available.

Is insurance affected if your driver drives my car?

Your own car insurance applies as normal. Our drivers are licensed professionals; for long assignments we can share driver documents for your records.
