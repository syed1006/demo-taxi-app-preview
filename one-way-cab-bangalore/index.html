One-Way Cab FAQs

What exactly is a one-way cab?

You pay only for the direction you travel — the fare covers your drop and the car returns empty at our cost. It's the cheapest way to relocate, catch a train or flight in another city, or travel when you don't need the cab to wait.

How is the one-way fare calculated?

One-way trips bill at a higher per-km rate than round trips (₹18/km for a sedan vs ₹13/km) because the car comes back empty — but you still save 30–40% versus paying a round trip you don't use. Popular corridors have fixed fares listed above, driver bata and fuel included.

Do you pick up from other cities back to Bangalore?

Yes — most corridors run both directions. A Mysore to Bangalore drop costs the same as Bangalore to Mysore. Tell us your pickup city on WhatsApp and we'll confirm a car and time.

Are tolls and driver bata included in one-way fares?

Driver bata and fuel are included in the fixed fares listed here. Tolls and inter-state permits (for Tamil Nadu, Andhra Pradesh or Kerala drops) are at actuals — the driver shows the receipts.

Can I book a one-way drop within Bangalore city?

Absolutely — point-to-point city drops are our bread and butter, day or night. Share pickup and drop on WhatsApp and we'll quote a fixed fare in minutes.
