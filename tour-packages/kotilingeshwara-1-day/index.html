Bangalore to Kotilingeshwara 1-Day Package

A devotional day trip east to Kammasandra in Kolar district, home of the Kotilingeshwara temple — its 33-metre (108-ft) Shiva linga is among the tallest anywhere, watched over by a 35-ft Nandi and surrounded by lakhs of smaller lingas installed by devotees over the decades. The 6 AM pickup gets you there for a calm mid-morning darshan before the day-trip crowds.

Itinerary

The price is all-inclusive on the car side — fuel, driver bata, tolls and parking — so your only spends are the temple offerings, any seva you book and lunch at Kolar on the way back. It's an easy, restful day: about two and a half hours each way with the whole afternoon at your pace.

Kotilingeshwara 1-Day FAQs

What does the ₹4,000 price include?

Car, fuel, driver bata, tolls and parking for the full day in a 4+1 sedan (₹5,500 for a 7+1). Temple offerings, seva bookings and meals are the only extras.

How far is Kotilingeshwara from Bangalore?

About 100 km east via NH-75 — two and a half hours each way. With the 6 AM pickup you're at the temple by about 8:45 AM, well before the midday rush.

Can we install a linga in our name?

Yes — that's the temple's tradition. Devotees can sponsor and install a linga through the temple counter; enquire there for the current seva details.

Can we add another stop on the way back?

Within the 250 included kilometres, gladly — the Kolaramma Temple in Kolar town or a drive past the KGF mining country are the usual picks.
