About Bangalore Urban Cabs

Bangalore Urban Cabs — ನಮ್ಮ ಸೇವೆ ("our service" in Kannada) — is a Bangalore-grown cab operator built on a simple promise: show up on time, drive safely, and never surprise anyone on price.

From our base in RMV 2nd Stage, Bhoopasandra, we run airport transfers, outstation trips across South India, hourly city rentals, corporate travel and fixed-price tour packages. Bookings work the way Bangalore actually communicates — a WhatsApp message, a confirmed fare in writing, and a driver whose details you have before pickup.

No surge pricing. No hidden charges. No call-centre runarounds. Just a fleet we maintain ourselves and drivers we'd trust with our own families.
