The Bangalore–Nandi Hills Drive

The classic Bangalore sunrise run. You head out on NH-44 past Hebbal and the airport, turn off near Devanahalli, and take the winding hill road up its forty-odd hairpin bends to the old fortress at 1,478 metres. With empty pre-dawn roads the drive takes about an hour and a half, so a 4:15–4:30 am pickup puts you at the gates when they open at 6 am, in time for sunrise over the cloud line at Tipu's Drop.

The hilltop was Tipu Sultan's summer retreat, and his restored lodge, the fort walls and the ancient Yoga Nandeeshwara temple give you plenty to walk through once the light comes up. Carry a light jacket — it is noticeably colder than the city, especially from November to February.

Weekends get busy fast: the hill road and parking choke by 8–8:30 am, so a weekday visit is far more relaxed. On the way down, stop at the 9th-century Bhoga Nandeeshwara temple in the base village, and if you want a full day, the 112-ft Adiyogi at Sadhguru Sannidhi is a short drive away.

Top Places to Visit in Nandi Hills

Tipu's Drop

Sheer 600-metre cliff and the hill's signature sunrise viewpoint above the cloud line.

Tipu's Summer Residence

Restored lodge where Tipu Sultan spent his summers, set inside the old fort walls.

Yoga Nandeeshwara Temple

Ancient Shiva temple at the summit, quiet and atmospheric in the early morning mist.

Amrita Sarovar

Perennial 'lake of nectar' near the top that once fed the fort's water channels.

Bhoga Nandeeshwara Temple

9th-century Dravidian temple complex in the base village — worth 45 minutes on the way down.

Nehru Nilaya

Colonial-era bungalow on the summit, once used by the Mysore commissioners and later by Nehru.

Travel Tips

  • Book a 4:15–4:30 am pickup for sunrise; gates open at 6 am and the best light is in the first hour.
  • Hilltop entry and parking fees are paid at the gate and are not part of the cab fare.
  • Carry a warm layer — the summit is several degrees colder than Bangalore, with strong wind at Tipu's Drop.
  • Avoid weekend mornings after 8 am; the hairpin road backs up badly and parking fills fast.
  • Monkeys are bold near the viewpoints — keep food and loose items inside the cab.

Bangalore to Nandi Hills — FAQs

What time should we start from Bangalore for the Nandi Hills sunrise?

A 4:15–4:30 am pickup works for most of the city. The drive takes about 90 minutes on empty roads, which puts you at the gates for the 6 am opening and at Tipu's Drop before the sun clears the horizon.

Is a one-way drop to Nandi Hills available?

Yes, a sedan one-way drop is ₹2,100. Most guests take the ₹3,300 round trip though, since return cabs are almost impossible to find at the hilltop and the driver waits through your visit.

Are tolls and entry fees included in the fare?

The fare covers fuel and driver bata. The NH-44 toll, the Nandi Hills entry fee and hilltop parking are extra and paid at actuals — together they add only a small amount.

How long do people usually spend at the top?

Two to three hours covers sunrise, Tipu's Drop, the summer residence and a chai at the summit stalls. Most guests are back in Bangalore comfortably before lunch.

Can we combine Nandi Hills with the Adiyogi statue?

Yes — Sadhguru Sannidhi is a short drive from the base of the hills. Do sunrise at the top, the Bhoga Nandeeshwara temple on the way down, and reach Adiyogi by evening for the light show; our Nandi Hills + Adiyogi one-day package covers exactly this.
