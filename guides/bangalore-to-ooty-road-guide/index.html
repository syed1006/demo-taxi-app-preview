Bangalore to Ooty by Road: Best Route, Bandipur Timings & Stops

Updated 29 July 2026 2 min read By the Bangalore Urban Cabs driver team

Ooty is the queen of South Indian hill stations, and the drive there is half the trip — eucalyptus air, a tiger-reserve crossing where spotted deer graze by the tarmac, and one of India’s most famous hairpin climbs. It’s also a drive with a hard rule that catches first-timers: the forest closes at night.

The route, in three acts

Act one: Bangalore → Mysore → Gundlupet (about 4 hours). The Mysore expressway does the heavy lifting; past Mysore you’re on the smooth Nanjangud–Gundlupet stretch. Breakfast before Mysore, fuel at Gundlupet.

Act two: the Bandipur crossing. From Gundlupet the highway runs straight through Bandipur Tiger Reserve — speed limits are enforced, stopping is prohibited, and sightings of deer, elephants and gaur from the car are genuinely common. The non-negotiable: the gate closes 9 PM to 6 AM. Every good Ooty plan is built around crossing in daylight, both ways.

Act three: the climb. Two choices from the forest side:

RouteCharacterBest for
Kalhatti (via Masinagudi)36 hairpins, steep, dramaticShorter; confident stomachs
Via GudalurLonger, gentler gradientKids, elders, full vehicles

On either climb the AC goes off for the steepest sections — standard ghat practice. Prone to motion sickness? Front seat, light breakfast, and take Gudalur.

Where to stop

  • Gundlupet’s sunflower fields (seasonal, post-monsoon) — the classic photo stop.
  • Bandipur reception zone — safari bookings if you’re overnighting at Masinagudi.
  • Pykara Lake and the Wenlock Downs “9th Mile” shooting spot — on the Ooty side, better saved for the return or a dedicated morning.

The honest plan

Leave Bangalore at 5:30–6 AM, cross Bandipur by early afternoon, and check in with the whole evening for the Botanical Gardens or the lake. Do Doddabetta, the tea museum and Coonoor the next morning, and start back by 2 PM to make the forest gate comfortably. That’s exactly how the Mysore + Ooty 3-Day Package is paced — ₹12,900 with the car, fuel and driver bata for all three days.

Quick answers

How far is Ooty from Bangalore and how long does it take?

About 270–290 km depending on route — realistically 7 to 8 hours door to door with a breakfast halt. A 5–6 AM start is the difference between a pleasant drive and an evening scramble against the forest gate.

What are the Bandipur night timings?

The highway through Bandipur Tiger Reserve closes to traffic from 9 PM to 6 AM, every day. Miss the window and you wait at the gate till morning — plan both crossings for daylight.

Which climb should we take — Kalhatti or Gudalur?

Kalhatti (via Masinagudi) is 36 steep hairpins and saves distance; it suits confident stomachs and light vehicles. The Gudalur route is longer but gentler — our default with kids, elders or a full car.

Is one day enough for Ooty?

No — it's a 7-hour drive each way with a forest gate curfew in between. Two days minimum; the 3-day Mysore + Ooty package is the version that doesn't feel rushed.
