Corporate Travel FAQs

Do you provide invoices for corporate bookings?

Yes — a consolidated monthly invoice with a trip-wise annexure (date, route, vehicle, fare). Per-trip invoices are also available if your process needs them.

Can you handle daily employee pickup rosters?

Yes. Share your roster and we assign regular drivers to fixed routes, with WhatsApp confirmations for every trip and monthly consolidated billing.

Is there a minimum monthly commitment?

No lock-ins. Start with per-trip billing; as volumes grow we'll propose retainer pricing that usually works out cheaper.

Which companies is this suited for?

Startups and SMEs that need reliable cabs without running a transport desk — typically 10 to 500 trips a month across employee transport, airport runs and client travel.
